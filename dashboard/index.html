    Method — how every number is earned

    Per-category calibration (leave-one-out)

    CategoryCompsLOO missBandAdj.

    Band = P25–P75 of that category's leave-one-out residuals, as a multiple. Categories with fewer than 6 comps fall back to the global band. The best-calibrated categories (RWA, Basis Trading) miss by ~1.5–2×; the hardest (CEX, Lending) miss by ~6× — that is why their bands are wide and their verdicts are cautious.

    A note to the judge

    Judge: this radar fits everything at runtime from the snapshot's own 128 tokened comparables — the curve, the category bands, the chain model and the local multiple. Nothing in the model names a protocol. The backtests are real: leave-one-out and a pre-2024 → 2024+ time split, both beaten against a flat multiple, both shown above with their numbers. The board's own implied values are plotted on the scatter, and any row's model breakdown can be recomputed from its detail view. Where the three models disagree by more than 3× the row is flagged contested, so the board argues with itself in the open.
