The rules

  • Ranked: records no token, TVL at or above $1M, and not a wrapped, staked or bridged product, not a bridge, liquid-staking, restaking or CEX protocol, not a versioned duplicate, not exchange-owned.
  • Watchlist (40) is forced in: every protocol on the house watchlist is ranked, whatever its implied value.
  • Sibling protocols — a first word shared with a protocol whose token already trades — are ranked at half value, not excluded: 4 sit on the board at ×0.5.
  • Excluded categories are read from the snapshot's own category field; versioned names end in V2, v2 or 2.0; wrapped and staked products carry the word in their name.
